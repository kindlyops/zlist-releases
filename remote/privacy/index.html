zlist Remote

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

zlist Remote is a remote control for zlist, the meeting playout app for Mac and Windows, published by Kindly Ops, LLC. It talks only to your own zlist, on your own local network, and never to us.

What we collect

Nothing. zlist Remote has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no servers of its own. We never see what you play, what your playlists hold, or how you use the app.

What stays on your device

  • Your pairings. For each computer you pair with, the app keeps its name, its zlist id, its network addresses and port, and the key and phone id zlist made for this phone. They are kept in the iOS keychain, on this device only, and are not included in backups to other devices.
  • The phone name you may type in Settings, kept in the app's settings on this device.
  • Cue pictures that zlist sends are held in memory while the app runs and are never saved.

What leaves your device

  • Messages to your zlist. The app finds your computer on the local network with Bonjour and connects to it directly, encrypted with the key from pairing. It sends your commands (play, stop, mute and so on), a keepalive, and the phone name from Settings (or the device model, such as “iPhone”), so zlist can list the phone in its settings. Nothing goes over the internet.

Camera and local network

  • Camera. The camera is used only while you scan zlist's pairing code. No picture is saved or sent anywhere.
  • Local network. iOS asks before the app may use your local network. Without it the app cannot reach zlist.

Children

zlist Remote is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page and in the app, with an updated effective date.
